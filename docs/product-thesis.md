# Product Thesis

## Problem

AI Dungeon Masters forget.

Campaign notes remember.

But campaign notes cannot reason.

As campaigns grow, continuity degrades.

Characters become inconsistent.

Plot threads disappear.

Rules are forgotten.

---

## Existing Solutions

### Notes

Remember information.

Cannot understand information.

### Wikis

Store information.

Cannot apply information.

### AI

Can reason.

Cannot reliably remember.

---

## Opportunity

Combine:

```text
Persistent Memory

+
Reasoning
```

into a single system.

---

## Solution

WorldKeeper acts as the continuity layer between:

* Players
* AI
* Campaigns

The AI reasons.

WorldKeeper remembers.

Together they maintain continuity across years of play.

---

## Core Value Proposition

WorldKeeper answers:

```text
What happened?

Who knows that?

Why does this matter?

What should I remember?
```

before attempting to answer:

```text
What should happen next?
```

---

## Success Metric

A campaign that lasts 100 sessions should feel as consistent as Session 1.

---

## Product Statement

WorldKeeper is a persistent world intelligence system that remembers the past, understands the present, and enables AI-assisted campaigns to remain coherent over time.

It is not a campaign wiki.

It is not a note-taking application.

It is the continuity layer between AI and long-running worlds.
